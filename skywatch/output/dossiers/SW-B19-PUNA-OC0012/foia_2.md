# Records about a surface change at -25.1072, -67.6410 (2022-05-05 to 2022-06-07)

**To:** The authority responsible for land, mining and environmental permits at this location in Argentina
**Re:** Request for records under the Ley 27.275 de Derecho de Acceso a la Información Pública

Dear Records Officer,

Under the Ley 27.275 de Derecho de Acceso a la Información Pública, I request copies of the records described below.

**Location:** latitude -25.10724, longitude -67.64098 (WGS84), and the area within 5 km of that point.
**Period:** between 2022-05-05 and 2022-06-07.

**Background.** Public satellite imagery (Copernicus Sentinel-2) shows a change in the ground surface of about 3,200 m² at this location in this period. I am trying to establish its ordinary cause and ask only for records that would help do so.

**Records requested:**
1. Permits, leases, rights-of-way, mining or exploration claims, and notices of operations covering the location.
2. Records of earthworks, prescribed burns, fire incidents, clean-ups or emergency responses at the location in the period.
3. Any survey, inspection or enforcement report that mentions ground disturbance at the location in the period.

**Format.** Electronic copies are preferred. If any part is withheld, please release the remainder and cite the exemption relied on for each redaction.

**Fees.** This request is made for research in the public interest and not for commercial use. I ask that fees be waived or, failing that, to be told the estimated cost before any is incurred.

I look forward to your reply within the statutory period (15 working days).
