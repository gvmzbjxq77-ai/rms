# Records about a surface change at -24.6781, 126.1436 (2024-10-05 to 2024-12-24)

**To:** The authority responsible for land, mining and environmental permits at this location in Australia
**Re:** Request for records under the Freedom of Information Act 1982 (Cth)

Dear Records Officer,

Under the Freedom of Information Act 1982 (Cth), I request copies of the records described below.

**Location:** latitude -24.67805, longitude 126.14364 (WGS84), and the area within 5 km of that point.
**Period:** between 2024-10-05 and 2024-12-24.

**Background.** Public satellite imagery (Copernicus Sentinel-2) shows a change in the ground surface of about 5,600 m² at this location in this period. I am trying to establish its ordinary cause and ask only for records that would help do so.

**Records requested:**
1. Permits, leases, rights-of-way, mining or exploration claims, and notices of operations covering the location.
2. Records of earthworks, prescribed burns, fire incidents, clean-ups or emergency responses at the location in the period.
3. Any survey, inspection or enforcement report that mentions ground disturbance at the location in the period.

**Format.** Electronic copies are preferred. If any part is withheld, please release the remainder and cite the exemption relied on for each redaction.

**Fees.** This request is made for research in the public interest and not for commercial use. I ask that fees be waived or, failing that, to be told the estimated cost before any is incurred.

I look forward to your reply within the statutory period (30 days).
