Does the method work? The control regions

Each control region holds something with a known, ordinary cause and a known date. The pipeline has to find it and then explain it. Three "pristine" controls were declared in advance to contain nothing, which measures how often the detector fires on empty ground.

Candidate catalogue

Every candidate that went through the elimination checks, least explained first. A tier describes how much evidence survives, never what the thing is. Open a row for the imagery, the measurements and every check that was run or could not be run.
